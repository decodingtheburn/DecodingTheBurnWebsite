If going on a strict, 100% carnivore diet feels too extreme or overwhelming to start with, you do not have to leap into the deep end right away.

This **BMS Low-Acid Low-Sugar Diet** is a gentle, highly achievable starting step. It is based directly on my personal health journey: when I was going through my **6th self-experiment episode**, this was pretty much the exact framework of foods I was eating.

---

## My Personal Experience: What Worked During Episode 6

When I first discovered that **acidity and fermentable sugar were the true environmental triggers** behind my burning mouth pain, I didn't immediately jump into a [pure zero-carb carnivore diet](/blog/1-my-bms-18-months-carnivore-experiment/).

Instead, I built a balanced, low-acid baseline diet. For a solid period during my 6th episode, eating low-acid meats, neutral plant seeds like hemp hearts, extra firm tofu, and mild low-acid vegetables (especially button mushrooms, which my mouth tolerated extremely well) kept my pain manageable and under control.

<PersonalEvolutionCards />

While this balanced low-acid approach did not last forever for me—I eventually transitioned to an [18-month carnivore diet](/blog/1-my-bms-18-months-carnivore-experiment/) to achieve an absolute, unbroken 0/10 pain baseline—it provided crucial relief when I needed it most. Depending on your personal nerve sensitivity and mucosal barrier status, this low-acid, low-sugar diet might be all you need to calm your burning pain, or at the very least, give you an easy transition to discover if acid and sugar are your primary triggers.

There are no guarantees in chronic pain, but this is what I empirically discovered through my own trials.

---

## Why Energy Density & Net Carbs Matter

When oral nerve pain spikes, eating becomes exhausting. Patients often restrict their food intake so severely that they end up malnourished, fatigued, and starving.

By prioritizing **high energy density (calories per 100g)** from zero-acid meats, neutral pH seeds & plant proteins, and low-sugar vegetables, you get maximum nutrition with minimal chewing and minimal mucosal contact time.

## Cooking Fats & Oils

Pure cooking oils and animal fats contain **zero sugar and zero carbohydrates**, meaning they cannot be fermented into oral acids by mouth bacteria. Adding healthy fats to your meals is one of the easiest ways to boost daily calories without triggering oral nerve pain.

## Sweet Treats & Desserts (Stevia-Based)

If you crave sweet flavors during your low-acid diagnostic trial, avoid artificial sweeteners (aspartame, sucralose) and fermentable sugar alcohols (xylitol, maltitol) which can trigger gut dysbiosis or mild acidity.

* **Pure Liquid Stevia (Organic Leaf Extract) 🍃:** Stevia is a zero-calorie, plant-derived glycoside that does **not ferment in the mouth** into oral acids.
* **Stevia-Sweetened Coconut Cream Dessert 🥥:** Whip full-fat organic coconut cream with 2–3 drops of pure liquid stevia and a dash of Ceylon cinnamon for a rich, zero-acid dessert.
* **Vanilla Stevia Whipped Ghee 🍨:** Whip room-temperature grass-fed ghee with vanilla extract and liquid stevia for a high-energy, zero-carb sweet treat.

---

## Herbal Teas (Zero Caffeine, Low Acid)

Avoid black tea, green tea, and coffee, which are acidic and contain caffeine that can elevate sympathetic nervous system stress. Instead, opt for soothing, neutral-to-alkaline herbal infusions:

* **Chamomile Tea 🌼 (pH ~6.0–6.5):** Anti-inflammatory and soothing for oral nerve endings.
* **Peppermint / Spearmint Tea 🌿 (pH ~6.2–6.8):** Natural cooling sensation via TRPM8 receptors without chemical acidity.
* **Lemon Balm / Rooibos ☕ (pH ~6.0–6.4):** Rich in antioxidants and completely caffeine-free.

## How to Test This Diet

1. **Commit to 7–14 Days:** Swap out all high-acid items (citrus, tomatoes, vinegar, coffee, sparkling water, wine) and high-sugar fruits for foods on this list.
2. **Log Your Daily Pain (0 to 10):** Note morning, afternoon, and evening pain levels in a simple notebook or app.
3. **Evaluate the Shift:** If your oral burning drops significantly, you have confirmed that **acid and sugar are key drivers of your pain**. From there, you can choose to stay on this balanced plan or experiment further with [carnivore](/blog/1-my-bms-18-months-carnivore-experiment/) if you want to push for a complete 0/10 baseline.
