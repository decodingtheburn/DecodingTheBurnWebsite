If you had told me years ago that I would be thriving on an all-meat diet, I would have thought you were crazy. 

To be completely honest, it took me a full year of trial and error just to adjust to it and mentally accept it. But today, I am completely pain-free, and I am so grateful that this way of eating&nbsp;exists.

<div class="part-label">Part 1</div>

## The Body Is an Amazing Machine

From my systems engineering and computer programming background, I look at the human body as an extraordinary machine. 

It never ceases to amaze me that our bodies are fully capable of nourishing us and keeping us strong with just meat, eggs, and seafood. 

I only wish I had known years ago the vital importance of teeth, saliva, and the continuous chemistry taking place in the mouth, in the gut, and in the bloodstream. I wish I had understood earlier what chronic acidity does to our tissues, and the true cost of the sugars we learned to love and gorge on from an early&nbsp;age.

## Why I Keep Going

Adjusting to an all-meat way of eating took patience, especially in the beginning when habits and cravings push&nbsp;back.

Once the oral burning stops and your body stabilizes, the choice becomes remarkably simple. For me, living free of pain and feeling energetic every single day makes this diet something I genuinely love and appreciate.

Every person's journey is unique, but sharing what has worked for my individual biology gives me hope that more of us can find answers and relief&nbsp;together.
