## Why We Are Asking
Sensitized teeth, cold/hot discomfort, and root sensitivity are direct markers of **exposed dentinal tubules** or thin cementum layers. If a high percentage of BMS patients also report adult tooth sensitivity, it strongly reinforces our theory that tubule exposure and fluid dynamics play a central role in BMS nociception.
