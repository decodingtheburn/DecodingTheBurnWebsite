Enoch Narciso's *Burning Mouth Syndrome: Diagnosis & Treatment* (2021) and follow-up volume *Burning Mouth Syndrome Symptoms* (2023) compile available academic literature, clinical trial findings, and symptom checklists into a self-published reference for sufferers seeking a broad factual summary of the disease.

### The Book Structure

Across roughly 250 pages, Narciso's volumes synthesize standard medical consensus into key topics:
* **Epidemiology and Risk Demographics:** Statistical prevalence of burning mouth, gender ratios, and common onset age ranges (predominantly perimenopausal and postmenopausal women).
* **Clinical Etiology Theories:** Summarizing scientific literature on central dopamine receptor hypoactivity, chorda tympani taste nerve hypofunction, and lingual nerve small-fiber neuropathy.
* **Pharmacological Review:** Documenting clinical trials covering clonazepam rinses, alpha-lipoic acid (ALA), capsaicin desensitization, and tricyclic antidepressants.
* **Non-Pharmacological Relief Strategies:** Basic dietary advice, avoidance of spicy and hot foods, and maintaining high daily hydration.

### What BMS Patients Can Learn

#### 1. A High-Level Compilation of Published Trials
For patients who want a consolidated summary of medical research without paying for expensive academic journal paywalls, Narciso collects the primary clinical drug trials that have been conducted on burning mouth over the past three decades.

#### 2. Understanding Alpha-Lipoic Acid Research
Narciso reviews the mixed medical evidence surrounding Alpha-Lipoic Acid (ALA), an antioxidant supplement frequently discussed in European clinical literature for peripheral neuropathy. He outlines the dosages studied (typically 600 mg daily) and explains why results vary widely across individual patients.

#### 3. Recognizing the Multi-Factorial Puzzle
Narciso's overview reinforces that burning mouth rarely has a single isolated cause in the literature. It frequently involves a convergence of sensory nerve vulnerability, taste system imbalance, and systemic hormonal transitions.

### Key Takeaway for Sufferers

Narciso's texts provide an introductory literature survey for patients who want a general overview of the medical treatments that have been formally tested in clinical studies.
