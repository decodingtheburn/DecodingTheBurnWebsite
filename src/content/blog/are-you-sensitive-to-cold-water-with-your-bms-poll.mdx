## 💡 Why This Question Matters

1. **Tubule Exposure vs. Mucosal Hypersensitivity:**
   Localized pain when cold water touches specific teeth suggests exposed dentinal tubules or focal root recession.
2. **Nerve Sensitization & Hydrodynamic Theory:**
   When dentin is exposed, cold water causes rapid fluid displacement inside dentinal tubules, triggering sharp nociceptive firing.
3. **Central vs. Peripheral Layering:**
   Understanding whether patients feel cold pain in specific spots versus across the entire mucosa helps distinguish local dental irritation from central neuropathic amplification.
