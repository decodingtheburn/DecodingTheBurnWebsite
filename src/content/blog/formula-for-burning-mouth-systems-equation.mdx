When doctors examine a burning mouth, they look for binary disease states: you either have an infection or you do not; you have an autoimmune antibody or you do not; your blood test is normal or abnormal. When everything tests negative, the condition is stamped as "idiopathic" or "psychogenic".

As a software programmer, I reject the black-box explanation. 

In computer systems, an alarm never triggers by magic. An alarm is simply the output of an evaluation function when incoming input signals cross an electrical threshold. The human nervous system operates under the exact same physics. 

Burning Mouth Syndrome is not an incomprehensible enigma. It is the predictable numerical result of an equation where physical acid loads and hardware leaks overwhelm local buffering capacity, tripping peripheral sensors that flood the trigeminal switchboard with pain telemetry.

Here is the engineering equation for the source of burning mouth&nbsp;pain.

## 2. Deconstructing the Numerator: What Drives the Fire

The numerator contains every factor that injects protons onto sensory nerve endings or increases their firing&nbsp;sensitivity:

### 1. Acid Load (&sum; [H<sup>+</sup>])
Protons (<span class="chem-formula">H<sup>+</sup></span>) are the primary physical fuel for the pain. This load comes from three cumulative sources:
* **Dietary Intake:** Direct exposure to low-pH foods and beverages (black coffee at pH 5.0, salad vinaigrettes at pH 3.2, citrus, sparkling waters, alcohol).
* **Subgingival Biofilm Fermentation:** Anaerobic bacteria (*Streptococcus mutans*, *P. gingivalis*) trapped in periodontal pockets ferment dietary carbohydrates into concentrated lactic acid. At the bottom of a 4&nbsp;mm gum pocket, micro-pH frequently drops to **4.5 to 5.0**.
* **Gastric Reflux (LPR / GERD):** Aerosolized pepsin and hydrochloric acid vapor pooling across the oral floor and palate during sleep.

### 2. Physical Exposure Area (A<sub>leak</sub>)
Acids cannot trigger pain through intact, impermeable crystalline enamel. They require a breach in the physical architecture:
* **Receded Root Margins (A<sub>tubules</sub>):** Every square millimeter of exposed root dentin contains **20,000 to 45,000 open fluid channels** (dentinal tubules) leading straight to pulpal A-delta and C-fibers.
* **Marginal Hardware Gaps (A<sub>gaps</sub>):** Ten-to-twenty-year-old composite resins and crown cement seams that have broken down, creating sub-micron gutters where capillary action wicks saliva downward.
* **Enamel Craze Lines (A<sub>craze</sub>):** Structural stress cracks in enamel that channel protons into underlying dentin.

### 3. Receptor Sensitization (D<sub>ASIC3</sub> &middot; S<sub>sens</sub>)
Recent electrophysiological research confirms that **100% of human dorsal root ganglion pain neurons express Acid-Sensing Ion Channels (ASIC3 and ASIC1a)**. When inflammatory mediators (such as arachidonic acid, substance P, or bradykinin) are present, the activation threshold of ASIC3 shifts in the alkaline direction, meaning receptors fire under extraordinarily mild pH drops (pH 7.0 to 6.3).

## 3. Deconstructing the Denominator: Your Biological Defense Shield

The denominator represents everything protecting your nerves from proton&nbsp;activation:

### 1. Salivary Flow & Bicarbonate Buffering (Q<sub>saliva</sub> &times; [<span class="chem-formula">HCO<sub>3</sub><sup>&minus;</sup></span>])
Saliva is not just water. It is a biological buffer engine:
* Resting saliva flow averages roughly **0.3 to 0.4 mL/min**, carrying modest levels of salivary bicarbonate (<span class="chem-formula">HCO<sub>3</sub><sup>&minus;</sup></span>).
* When salivary output drops (due to medications, Sjogren's syndrome, post-menopausal hormonal shifts, or nocturnal mouth breathing), the denominator collapses toward zero. 
* Any fraction with a shrinking denominator causes the total value to spike exponentially. This explains why BMS pain overwhelmingly escalates late in the afternoon or evening as cumulative dehydration sets&nbsp;in.

### 2. Central Gate Inhibition (G<sub>inhibition</sub>)
According to the Gate Control Theory of Pain, fast-conducting, myelinated A&beta; mechanoreceptors (stimulated by chewing food, pressure, or physical movement) activate inhibitory interneurons in the brainstem (*Subnucleus Caudalis*). 

This closes the synaptic gate against slow, unmyelinated C-fiber burning telemetry. This explains why **chewing gum or eating a meal temporarily silences burning mouth pain**, only for the burning to return thirty minutes later once chewing ceases.
