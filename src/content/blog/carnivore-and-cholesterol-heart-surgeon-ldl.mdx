## The Conventional Panic: "Your LDL Tripled"

Kerry shared that after six months of eating an all-meat carnivore diet, his bloodwork appeared alarming under conventional lab reference ranges. His LDL had tripled, eventually reaching **195 mg/dL**. His primary care physician was alarmed, warning of clogged arteries and prescribing immediate statins.

Yet, clinically and physically, Kerry felt the best he had in his entire life. He had shed visceral body fat, eliminated systemic joint aches, and normalized his energy.

Why is there such a massive disconnect between how a patient feels on a low-carb carnivore diet and what conventional blood panels flag as dangerous?

## 1. What LDL Actually Does: The Fuel Delivery Fleet

As Dr. Ovadia points out, Low-Density Lipoprotein (LDL) is not a toxic waste product or a poison. It is a biological transport vehicle.

* **On a Standard Carbohydrate Diet:** Your cells run primarily on glucose. Triglycerides and cholesterol circulate alongside insulin, with excess energy stored as glycogen and adipose tissue.
* **On a Zero-Carb Carnivore Diet:** When you eliminate dietary carbohydrates and seed oils, your liver shifts your entire metabolism into fat-burning mode. To deliver fatty acids and energy to muscle and organ cells that require fuel, your liver packages triglycerides into Very-Low-Density Lipoproteins (VLDL).
* **The Energy Bus Model:** As VLDLs circulate and unload their fatty acids (energy), they naturally turn into LDL particles. In an athletic, lean, or fat-adapted individual, higher circulating LDL is often simply a sign of high-output **fat trafficking**, not vascular pathology.

This biological phenomenon is now formally recognized in the scientific literature as the **Lean Mass Hyper-Responder (LMHR)** triad:
1. **Elevated LDL-C:** Typically &gt;200 mg/dL (or significant percentage surges).
2. **High HDL-C:** Often &gt;80 mg/dL (protective, anti-inflammatory "good" cholesterol).
3. **Plummeting Triglycerides:** Frequently &lt;70 mg/dL (indicating zero insulin resistance or fatty liver).

## 2. Dr. Ovadia's Core Distinction: Metabolic Health vs. Isolated LDL

As a heart surgeon who has physically operated on thousands of human hearts, Dr. Philip Ovadia highlights a reality that standard clinical exams often miss:

> **Half of all heart attack patients admitted to hospitals have completely normal or low LDL cholesterol.**

If LDL were the sole cause of arterial plaque, everyone with high LDL would develop atherosclerosis, and everyone with low LDL would be immune. In clinical reality, vascular damage requires an environment of **systemic chronic inflammation, endothelial injury, and insulin resistance**.

To evaluate whether high LDL is truly dangerous in an individual, Dr. Ovadia emphasizes looking at five markers of metabolic health:

1. **Triglyceride-to-HDL Ratio:** An ideal ratio is under 2.0 (and ideally close to 1.0). When triglycerides are low and HDL is high, it reflects minimal small, dense, oxidized LDL particles.
2. **Fasting Insulin &amp; HbA1c:** Fasting insulin below 5 &mu;IU/mL and HbA1c below 5.4% confirm that the vascular endothelium is not being battered by chronic hyperinsulinemia and advanced glycation end-products (AGEs).
3. **Blood Pressure:** Normal resting blood pressure ensures arteries are not subjected to mechanical shear stress.
4. **Waist-to-Height Ratio:** Maintaining a waist circumference under half your height confirms absence of visceral organ fat.
5. **High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein (hs-CRP):** A systemic inflammation marker; when hs-CRP is &lt;0.5 mg/L, the body is in a calm, non-inflamed immunological state.

---

## 3. Direct Imaging: The Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) Scan

Rather than guessing vascular risk based on a single surrogate blood number, Dr. Ovadia strongly advocates for **objective visual confirmation**: the **Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) scan**.

* A CAC scan is a low-radiation, inexpensive CT scan of the heart that directly images whether calcified plaque exists inside the coronary arteries.
* A score of **Zero (CAC = 0)** confirms that despite whatever LDL number is circulating in the bloodstream, no active calcified plaque has formed in the vessel walls.
* Serial CAC scanning or soft-plaque CT angiograms provide definitive proof of whether dietary changes are causing disease progression or maintaining clean arteries.

## 4. How This Connects to Burning Mouth Syndrome &amp; Chronic Pain

For people using a carnivore or strict zero-carb elimination diet to calm Burning Mouth Syndrome, this conversation offers vital perspective:

1. **Systemic Inflammation Is the True Nerve Enemy:** High blood sugar, seed oils, and subgingival lactic acid fermentation continuously gate Acid-Sensing Ion Channels (ASIC3) and TRPV1 receptors. Eliminating carbohydrates removes the chemical fuel that drives peripheral oral burning.
2. **Context Matters More Than Isolated Numbers:** When you eliminate chronic pain, lower systemic inflammation, and normalize blood glucose, an increase in circulating LDL is often a benign physiological adaptation to fat-fueled energy metabolism.
3. **Partner With Informed Clinicians:** If your doctor expresses concern over your post-carnivore lipid panel, ask for comprehensive metabolic testing (Fasting Insulin, ApoB, hs-CRP) and request a CAC scan rather than accepting a knee-jerk prescription.
