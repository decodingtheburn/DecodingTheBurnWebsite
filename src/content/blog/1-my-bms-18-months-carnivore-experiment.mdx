## Who Am I?

I am a Burning Mouth Syndrome (BMS) patient. When my mouth first started to burn, my entire world collapsed into a single obsession: making the burning stop. For four years, I lived through a vicious cycle of mysterious flares, temporary relief windows, and devastating regressions. Today I am pain-free, hovering between 0/10 and 1/10, and I want to share what I have&nbsp;discovered.

My goal with this post is to document the history of my pain so I can find the right dentists and doctors. When all my observations, timelines, and tests are written down, clinicians can understand my situation in a much better way than if everything is trapped in my&nbsp;head.

I am conducting my own ongoing research and documenting my journey so I can minimize, reduce, or eliminate the pain. Make it simple: this is fundamentally for myself, but as a secondary goal, I want to help others as well who are navigating the same chronic burning. If sharing my data can save someone else months of confusion, it is well worth&nbsp;it.

I believe that if patients, doctors, and dentists all work together, the only way to help advance the research is by talking about it and getting people to work together as a&nbsp;team.

## My BMS Experiments

Here is the complete chronological progression of my Burning Mouth Syndrome journey, from the first trigger approximately 4 years ago to the all-acid discovery and carnivore reset 18 months ago and my stable zero-pain baseline today.

<InteractiveProgressionChart />

Looking at the graph above, you can see that my journey was not a straight downward line. It was an escalating series of distinct episodes, each followed by a temporary window where I thought I had solved the puzzle.

### Experiment #1: Eliminate Beer & Wine (Approx. 4 Years&nbsp;Ago)
My symptoms first began with severe aching and burning triggered by dark ales, beer, and wine. At the time, I had no idea what Burning Mouth Syndrome was. I assumed it was an isolated sensitivity. I eliminated alcohol completely, and within weeks, the burning dropped back down to zero. For **~6 months**, I was completely pain-free. I thought it was over.

### Experiment #2: Eliminate Coffee
Six months later, out of nowhere, the fire returned at an 8/10. This time, the culprit was daily coffee. The acidity and roasting compounds irritated the oral mucosa. Once again, I eliminated coffee entirely, and the burning subsided back to a 0/10 baseline for another **~6 months pain-free**.

### Experiment #3: Routine Dental Cleaning
The third flare was triggered following a routine dental cleaning. Subgingival scaling and bacterial disruption aggravated my underlying gingival recession pockets. Once the gums settled, I entered another **~6-month pain-free window**.

### Experiment #4: Eliminate Most Fruit
In late 2024, healthy fruits (citrus, berries, apples) became intolerable. The natural plant acids (pH 3.0) stung exposed nerve endings like lemon juice on an open cut. Eliminating most fruit restored my zero-pain baseline for another **~6 months pain-free**.

### Experiment #5: Cervical Composite Patches
By early 2025, the pain escalated to a severe 9/10. My dentist identified root exposure and placed **cervical composite bonding patches** to physically shield the sensitive dentin. This mechanical barrier worked brilliantly, giving me another **~6 months of complete pain-free relief**.

### Experiment #6: Eliminate All Fruits, Grains & Vegetables (The Carnivore Reset)
In April 2025, the composite patches degraded from micro-leakage and acid wear. The burning roared back at a catastrophic **10/10**. 

At this point, every conventional food was an enemy. Vegetables, grains, teas, and sauces all caused excruciating flares. Out of sheer necessity, I made the radical switch to an all-meat, zero-acid carnivore baseline. Within 7 to 14 days, the 10/10 agony dropped down to a quiet 2/10, and eventually down to a stable 0/10&nbsp;baseline.

### Experiment #7: Testing 50+ Supplements & Water Filtration (Nothing Worked)
Before discovering the root cause, I went through an exhausting medical gauntlet. I consulted **4 different dentists and 2 medical doctors**, hoping someone could find a physical reason for the relentless fire. None of the clinical exams, swabs, or standard blood panels revealed an answer. 

In desperate search of relief, I systematically tested **over 50 vitamins, minerals, electrolytes, bile salts, and herbal supplements**, along with zero-TDS water filtration gathered from medical literature and patient communities:

## My Theory

My theory is that the carnivore diet, just like mushrooms, has no sugar and no carbohydrates. Because there is zero fermentable sugar, it cannot ferment in the mouth. 

When oral bacteria consume carbohydrates, even the healthy complex starches in vegetables and grains, they convert them directly into corrosive lactic acid. 

In my body, **the true environmental trigger seems to be acid.**

### How Acid Burns: Exposed Hardware & Dentinal Conduits

Healthy teeth are sealed by an enamel crown, but over decades of brushing, chewing forces, and periodontal shifts, gums recede. Once gum tissue recedes below the enamel margin, it leaves the porous root dentin exposed. 

<TubuleRecessionDiagram />

### The Trigeminal Connection: Acid-Sensing Ion Channels (ASICs)

Dentin is not a solid wall. It is honeycombed with **20,000 to 45,000 microscopic fluid-filled dentinal tubules per square millimeter**. A single human tooth houses approximately **3 to 5 million tubules**, adding up to **over 100 million micro-channels across the entire mouth**.

When gum recession exposes root surfaces or micro-margins develop around old dental work, oral acids wash over these open conduits. 

Acids strip away the microscopic mineral smear plugs sealing the tubule apertures. This causes rapid hydrodynamic fluid shifts and acidic chemical diffusion straight into the dental pulp, where acid protons (H⁺) bind directly to **Acid-Sensing Ion Channels (ASICs)** on peripheral Trigeminal nerve endings.

### Radiating Pain & Central Convergence: Why the Entire Face Burns

At the peak of my burning episodes, the pain was not confined to a single tooth or the tip of my tongue. My entire mouth felt ablaze. The hard palate (roof of my mouth) felt scalded, my nasal passages burned with dry heat, my gums ached, and shooting pain radiated into my ear canals.

This occurs because all sensory fibers from the teeth, gums, tongue, palate, and nose converge onto the same second-order neurons in the brainstem (**the Subnucleus Caudalis of the Trigeminal Spinal Nucleus**). 

When millions of pulpal and lingual ASIC3 channels fire non-stop from acid exposure, the central brainstem hub enters a state of **central sensitization**. The neural signals "spill over" and radiate along all three branches of Cranial Nerve V:

### First Leak Found Under a Crown

Six months ago, I had a failing lower crown with three hidden cavities underneath. When my dentist removed the compromised crown, treated the decayed margins, and sealed the tooth with a new restoration, **the persistent chronic pain in my lower jaw vanished.**

This proved to me that my burning sensation was not a phantom mystery in my brain. It was a physical hardware defect communicating with localized chemical irritants.

### The 72-Hour Latency Trap & Amnesia

Once I reached a stable 2/10 on strict carnivore, human psychology worked against&nbsp;me.

When you are at a 10/10, you promise yourself you will eat plain ground beef and salt forever. But once you sit at a calm, pain-free baseline for a few weeks, **your brain forgets how bad the fire was.**

I would walk into the grocery store feeling fantastic. I would pass the snack aisles and start rationalizing:

> *"A bag of popcorn isn't acidic. Popcorn is gentle, right? And what about some cooked vegetables? Vegetables are healthy. A little bit won't hurt me."*

I bought them. I ate them. And every single time, I triggered the **72-hour latency trap**.

If eating vegetables or popcorn caused immediate burning within five minutes, I would have stopped immediately. But BMS operates with a delayed biochemical&nbsp;latency:

1. **Day 1 (The Cheat):** I ate the off-plan food. My mouth felt completely fine. I went to bed thinking I was permanently healed.
2. **Day 2 (The Smolder):** A mild tingling returned to the tip of my tongue (3/10 pain).
3. **Day 3 (The Full Flare):** The inflammatory cascade peaked. The burning surged back up to a **5/10**. 
4. **Days 4 to 6 (The Meat Reset):** In panic and regret, I returned to strict meat, salt, and water. Within 72 hours, the inflammation calmed back down to 0/10 or 1/10.

As shown on the progression graph, I repeated this exact yo-yo cycle over **one full year of testing and cheating** before finally locking in **six months of unbroken strict carnivore**.

## The Turning Point: Radical Acceptance

After a full year of riding this emotional and physical roller coaster, I had an honest conversation with myself. 

The mental exhaustion of anticipating Day 3 flares was far worse than the discipline of sticking to plain meat. I had to practice **radical acceptance**: my oral tissues and sensitized nerve pathways simply do not tolerate plant acids, oxalates, or fermentable carbohydrates.

For the past six months, I locked in my strict zero-negotiation foundation:
* **Core Nutrition:** 100% strict ruminant meat (beef, lamb, pork, poultry, fish, eggs, mushrooms) and clean animal fats.
* **Neutral Hydration:** Pure water and mineral salt only.
* **Zero Cheats:** Six continuous months at a rock-solid 0/10 pain baseline.

## The Cheese Nuance

Through careful post-baseline testing, I discovered one subtle exception: hard cheeses.
* **1 meal with cheese:** Zero pain flare.
* **2 meals with cheese in one day:** Causes a minor, temporary jump from 0/10 to 1/10.

Because a 1/10 is barely perceptible compared to the living hell of a 10/10, I occasionally accept that small trade-off knowingly. The difference today is **conscious control** instead of unpredictable misery.

## Dentist Visits

Eliminating dietary acids extinguished the chemical fire, but the underlying hardware vulnerabilities still remain in my mouth. 

Over the next few months, my diagnostic plan is focused on structural dental verification:
1. **Check Cervical Composite Patches:** Evaluate the composite resin seals on my exposed root recessions to verify whether microleakage or bonding degradation is occurring.
2. **Examine Micro-Infractions & Enamel Craze Lines:** Inspect my molars for hairline micro-cracks that allow oral fluids to bypass enamel.
3. **Assess 40-Year-Old Silver Amalgam & Composite Fillings:** Check the margins of my old restorations with magnification and transillumination to detect hidden marginal gaps or secondary micro-decay.

I will document every dental appointment, patch adjustment, and clinical measurement, and report my findings back to this community.

## Conclusion & Takeaways

These observations are based on my personal N=1 self-experiments. Burning Mouth Syndrome is complex, and I am not prescribing a rigid dietary dogma for everyone. Instead, consider these three principles:

1. **Monitor & Notice Acid Reactions:** You do not have to overhaul your entire life overnight. Simply start by observing how your oral burning behaves over 48 to 72 hours when you temporarily remove high-acid items (sodas, citrus, wine, coffee) or fermentable starches. Notice the patterns in your own body.
2. **Starve the Chemical Fire First:** When tissues are actively inflamed at an 8/10 or 10/10, nerves fire erratically. Lowering acidic chemical contact allows the peripheral trigeminal nerve to rest and makes subsequent diagnostic tests far more accurate.
3. **Find a Collaborative Dental Specialist:** Look beyond surface-level exams for subtle physical hardware defects: root recessions, exposed dentinal tubules, failing restoration margins, and hairline enamel infractions.
