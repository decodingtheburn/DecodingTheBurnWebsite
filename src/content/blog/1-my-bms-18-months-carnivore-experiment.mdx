## My Theory on Why the Carnivore Diet Works

My theory on why the carnivore diet works comes down to one simple fact: meat has no sugar. Because there is zero sugar, oral bacteria have nothing to ferment in the mouth.

My main discovery is that vegetables, just like fruits and grains, have sugar in them.

We know from FODMAP research that short-chain carbohydrates and sugar alcohols ferment rapidly. But even everyday savory vegetables are loaded with simple sugars and starches. When we chew vegetables, naturally occurring bacteria in the mouth feed on those sugars and convert them directly into acid.

That acid is what washes over our teeth and gums, directly triggering the pain sensors in our mouth and setting off the burning fire. By eliminating sugars and plant acids through a carnivore baseline, bacteria can no longer produce that acid, allowing irritated oral tissues and sensitized nerves to calm down completely.

In my body, **the true environmental trigger is acid.**

## 7 Experiments That Lowered My Pain Over The Last Four Years

Looking at the graph below, you can see that my journey was not a straight downward line. It was an escalating series of distinct episodes over four years, each followed by a temporary window where I thought I had solved the puzzle.

Here is how I discovered that acid seems to be a problem for my pain, and how eliminating it completely allowed the burning to go&nbsp;away:

### Experiment #1: Eliminate Beer & Wine (Approx. 4 Years&nbsp;Ago)
My symptoms first began with severe aching and burning triggered by dark ales, beer, and wine. At the time, I had no idea what Burning Mouth Syndrome was. I assumed it was an isolated sensitivity. I eliminated alcohol completely, and within weeks, the burning dropped back down to zero. For **~6 months**, I was completely pain-free. I thought it was over.

### Experiment #2: Eliminate Coffee
Six months later, out of nowhere, the fire returned at an 8/10. This time, the culprit was daily coffee. The acidity and roasting compounds irritated the oral mucosa. Once again, I eliminated coffee entirely, and the burning subsided back to a 0/10 baseline for another **~6 months pain-free**.

### Experiment #3: Routine Dental Cleaning
The third flare was triggered following a routine dental cleaning. Subgingival scaling and bacterial disruption aggravated my underlying gingival recession pockets. Once the gums settled, I entered another **~6-month pain-free window**.

### Experiment #4: Eliminate Most Fruit
In late 2024, healthy fruits (citrus, berries, apples) became intolerable. The natural plant acids (pH 3.0) stung exposed nerve endings like lemon juice on an open cut. Eliminating most fruit restored my zero-pain baseline for another **~6 months pain-free**.

### Experiment #5: Gumline Bonding Patches
By early 2025, the pain escalated to a severe 9/10. My dentist identified root exposure and abfractions at the gumline and placed **gumline composite bonding patches** (cervical dental resin) to physically shield the sensitive dentin. This mechanical barrier worked brilliantly, giving me another **~6 months of complete pain-free relief**.

### Experiment #6: Two-Week Carnivore Trial
In April 2025, the composite patches degraded from micro-leakage and acid wear. The burning roared back at a catastrophic **10/10**. At that point, I had no idea what to eat anyway; almost every conventional food seemed to set my mouth on&nbsp;fire.

One day, I was browsing on YouTube and saw a suggested video about the carnivore diet and inflammation. I didn't know anything about the carnivore diet, and I had never even heard of it before, but out of curiosity, I clicked on it. That's when I discovered an entire community of people who eat only meat. 

It sounded strange to me at first, but living with constant 10/10 pain, I figured: *Let me give this a shot for a few days or a few weeks.* I didn't have a structured plan or know if it would even work, but I had nothing to lose. 

To my complete astonishment, within 7 to 10 days of eating only meat, my pain dropped down from a 10/10 to roughly a **3 out of 10**.

I was so relieved that I immediately thought: *Oh my god, my pain is so low now, maybe I'm cured!*

So I would go back to the grocery store, buy the typical foods I used to eat, and eat them. For about two days, things seemed fine, and then the excruciating burning would come roaring back. I would panic and scramble back to meat, salt, and water.

I repeated this exact **yo-yo dance for over a full year**. 

Looking back, my theory is that our daily food habits are incredibly powerful and I was unconsciously addicted to the sugars in the foods I used to eat. I kept convincing myself that a little bit of normal food would not hurt. 

Living through that yo-yo cycle was deeply stressful. Every time I switched back to an all-meat baseline, I spent days agonizing over whether the pain would actually drop back down, or if one day I would get permanently stuck in a 10/10 flare. 

This repeated pattern convinced me that acid was entering the pulp of my teeth, either through open tubules via hydrodynamic pressure or through a leaking dental restoration. And as I later discovered, **I certainly did have a leaking crown** with hidden cavities underneath.

### Experiment #7: Strict Carnivore for Six Months (Stable 0/10 Baseline)
After a full year of yo-yo testing and seeing the same 72-hour flare pattern repeat, I committed to 100% strict carnivore with zero cheats. 

For the past **six continuous months**, I have flatlined at a rock-solid **0/10 pain baseline**. By keeping oral bacteria completely starved of fermentable carbohydrates and eliminating all plant acids, my nerve pathways stabilized and the burning stopped entirely.

### How Acid Burns: Exposed Hardware & Dentinal Conduits

Healthy teeth are sealed by an enamel crown, but over decades of brushing, chewing forces, and periodontal shifts, gums recede. Once gum tissue recedes below the enamel margin, it leaves the porous root dentin exposed. 

<div id="tubule-recession-diagram-box">
<TubuleRecessionDiagram />
</div>

### The Trigeminal Connection: Acid-Sensing Ion Channels (ASICs)

Dentin is not a solid wall. It is honeycombed with **20,000 to 45,000 microscopic fluid-filled dentinal tubules per square millimeter**. A single human tooth houses approximately **3 to 5 million tubules**, adding up to **over 100 million micro-channels across the entire mouth**.

When gum recession exposes root surfaces or micro-margins develop around old dental work, oral acids wash over these open conduits. 

Acids strip away the microscopic mineral smear plugs sealing the tubule apertures. This causes rapid hydrodynamic fluid shifts and acidic chemical diffusion straight into the dental pulp, where acid protons (H⁺) bind directly to **Acid-Sensing Ion Channels (ASICs)** on peripheral Trigeminal nerve endings.

### Radiating Pain & Central Convergence: Why the Entire Face Burns

At the peak of my burning episodes, the pain was not confined to a single tooth or the tip of my tongue. My entire mouth felt ablaze. The hard palate (roof of my mouth) felt scalded, my nasal passages burned with dry heat, my gums ached, and shooting pain radiated into my ear canals.

This occurs because all sensory fibers from the teeth, gums, tongue, palate, and nose converge onto the same second-order neurons in the brainstem (**the Subnucleus Caudalis of the Trigeminal Spinal Nucleus**). 

When millions of pulpal and lingual ASIC3 channels fire non-stop from acid exposure, the central brainstem hub enters a state of **central sensitization**. The neural signals "spill over" and radiate along all three branches of Cranial Nerve V:

### The 72-Hour Latency Trap & Amnesia

Once I reached a stable 2/10 on strict carnivore, human psychology worked against&nbsp;me.

When you are at a 10/10, you promise yourself you will eat plain ground beef and salt forever. But once you sit at a calm, pain-free baseline for a few weeks, **your brain forgets how bad the fire was.**

I would walk into the grocery store feeling fantastic. I would pass the snack aisles and start rationalizing:

> *"A bag of popcorn isn't acidic. Popcorn is gentle, right? And what about some cooked vegetables? Vegetables are healthy. A little bit won't hurt me."*

I bought them. I ate them. And every single time, I triggered the **72-hour latency trap**.

If eating vegetables or popcorn caused immediate burning within five minutes, I would have stopped immediately. But BMS operates with a delayed biochemical&nbsp;latency:

1. **Day 1 (The Cheat):** I ate the off-plan food. My mouth felt completely fine. I went to bed thinking I was permanently healed.
2. **Day 2 (The Smolder):** A mild tingling returned to the tip of my tongue (3/10 pain).
3. **Day 3 (The Full Flare):** The inflammatory cascade peaked. The burning surged back up to a **5/10**. 
4. **Days 4 to 6 (The Meat Reset):** In panic and regret, I returned to strict meat, salt, and water. Within 72 hours, the inflammation calmed back down to 0/10 or 1/10.

As shown on the progression graph, I repeated this exact yo-yo cycle over **one full year of testing and cheating** before finally locking in **six months of unbroken strict carnivore**.

### The Turning Point: Radical Acceptance

After a full year of riding this emotional and physical roller coaster, I had an honest conversation with myself. 

The mental exhaustion of anticipating Day 3 flares was far worse than the discipline of sticking to plain meat. I had to practice **radical acceptance**: my oral tissues and sensitized nerve pathways simply do not tolerate plant acids, oxalates, or fermentable carbohydrates.

For the past six months, I locked in my strict zero-negotiation foundation:
* **Core Nutrition:** 100% strict ruminant meat (beef, lamb, pork, poultry, fish, eggs, mushrooms) and clean animal fats.
* **Neutral Hydration:** Pure water and mineral salt only.
* **Zero Cheats:** Six continuous months at a rock-solid 0/10 pain baseline.

### The Cheese Nuance

Through careful post-baseline testing, I discovered one subtle exception: hard cheeses.
* **1 meal with cheese:** Zero pain flare.
* **2 meals with cheese in one day:** Causes a minor, temporary jump from 0/10 to 1/10.

Because a 1/10 is barely perceptible compared to the living hell of a 10/10, I occasionally accept that small trade-off knowingly. The difference today is **conscious control** instead of unpredictable misery.
