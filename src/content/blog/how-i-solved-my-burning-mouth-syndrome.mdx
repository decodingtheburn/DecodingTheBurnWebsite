My journey from an unexpected initial trigger to complete remission took three years of forensic investigation, trial-and-error, and systematic dietary&nbsp;elimination:

## The Hidden Dental Discovery: Tooth 36 and Three&nbsp;Cavities

Removing acids and sugars brought daily relief, but I still needed to understand what physical trigger had ignited the nerve in the first&nbsp;place.

About six months into carnivore, I went for a cleaning at a dental hygienist school. An experienced dentist there looked closely and spotted what appeared to be an apical lesion beneath an old crown on tooth&nbsp;36.

I immediately went to a dental clinic to have tooth 36 evaluated, and the clinician agreed to section and replace the old&nbsp;crown.

When they removed the crown, they uncovered an alarming reality: three active cavities had been rotting underneath that crown for years, completely invisible on standard 2D dental&nbsp;x-rays!

Five dentists had examined my mouth during my journey, and every single one of them had missed this defective crown and hidden decay. Because they did not know what to look for, the infection had continued festering undetected beneath the&nbsp;surface.

The dentist excavated all three cavities, treated the tooth, and placed a brand-new, tightly sealed crown. Once that hidden decay was removed, the deep chronic pain in my lower jaw stopped&nbsp;completely.

Combined with gentle brushing habits, my canine sensitivity has settled down, leaving only mild sensitivity when I floss deeply.

## The Biophysical Takeaway: Fuel vs.&nbsp;Hardware

Looking back, my journey revealed that Burning Mouth Syndrome was not a mysterious psychosomatic curse. It was an electrochemical fire sustained by two distinct&nbsp;drivers:

1. **The Systemic Fuel (Dietary Acids & Sugars):** Plant acids directly activate acid-sensing ion channels (ASIC1 and ASIC3) and heat-pain receptors. Eliminating plant acids and sugars stopped bacterial fermentation and starved the nerve&nbsp;fire.

2. **The Dental Hardware (Infection & Tubule Exposure):** Hidden decay under tooth 36 fed bacterial toxins directly into my trigeminal nerve. Excavating the decay and replacing the crown permanently removed that physical&nbsp;spark.

## Independent Research & Patient&nbsp;Advocacy

I am sharing my lived experience as an independent literature researcher and patient advocate. I am not a physician, dentist, or dietitian, and this account is a personal narrative rather than medical&nbsp;advice.

Strict elimination diets like carnivore carry unique considerations. Anyone exploring dietary changes should do their own research and consult a qualified healthcare&nbsp;professional.

What allowed my personal nerve pathways to heal may not be the identical root cause for every person.

Still, my recovery demonstrates that when you systematically track symptoms and investigate biophysical causes, answers can be&nbsp;found.
