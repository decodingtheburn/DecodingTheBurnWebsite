If you're suffering from Burning Mouth Syndrome (BMS), I understand the suffering that you're going through because I had severe symptoms for three years. Along the way, there was a lot of things that I tried that didn't work, which was deeply disheartening. But through relentless persistence and some sheer luck, I stumbled across things that actually did&nbsp;work. Today, I have been 18 months pain-free (0/10 to 1/10), and I think I have found a permanent solution (more to come later in this&nbsp;article).

*Disclaimer:* I know that what worked for me might not work for everyone. Always consult with your physician before trying anything&nbsp;new.

In this post, I want to share a bit about my journey: what worked, what didn't work, my understanding of the disease and its triggers, the root cause that I found for my own situation, and the personal literature research I conducted. Most importantly, I want to share how a targeted elimination diet can help people reduce their symptoms, and maybe for some like me, bring the burning down to a very low&nbsp;minimum.

## My Personal Mission

Connecting patients, dentists, doctors, and researchers together to find solutions that will work, and sharing information so we can make progress and discoveries into solutions for this debilitating&nbsp;condition.

## The Continued Dental Search: Root Canals, the Hygiene School, and Jewish General Hospital

Removing dietary acids and sugars brought complete daily relief by starving the biochemical fire. But as an engineer, I knew that dietary fuel was only half of the equation. I still needed to uncover what physical hardware fault had ignited the nerve in the first&nbsp;place.

I went back to several dental specialists to continue my investigation with relentless&nbsp;precision:

### Checking My Existing Root Canals
Before focusing on newer restorations, I wanted to rule out deep hidden infections. I went to have my two existing root-canal-treated teeth (teeth 46 and 47) thoroughly checked. High-resolution radiographic evaluation confirmed that both roots were dense, well-obturated, and free of periapical inflammation. They were completely ruled&nbsp;out.

### The Dental Hygiene School Discovery (Cégep Édouard-Montpetit)
About six months into carnivore remission, I went for a routine cleaning at the dental hygiene clinic of Cégep Édouard-Montpetit. An observant dentist supervising the clinic looked closely at my lower left jaw and spotted what appeared to be an apical lesion beneath an old crown on tooth&nbsp;36.
