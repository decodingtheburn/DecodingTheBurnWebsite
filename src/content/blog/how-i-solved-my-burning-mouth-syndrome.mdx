## The Continued Dental Search: Root Canals, the Hygiene School, and Jewish General Hospital

Removing dietary acids and sugars brought complete daily relief by starving the biochemical fire. But as an engineer, I knew that dietary fuel was only half of the equation. I still needed to uncover what physical hardware fault had ignited the nerve in the first&nbsp;place.

I went back to several dental specialists to continue my investigation with relentless&nbsp;precision:

### Checking My Existing Root Canals
Before focusing on newer restorations, I wanted to rule out deep hidden infections. I went to have my two existing root-canal-treated teeth (teeth 46 and 47) thoroughly checked. High-resolution radiographic evaluation confirmed that both roots were dense, well-obturated, and free of periapical inflammation. They were completely ruled&nbsp;out.

### The Dental Hygiene School Discovery
About six months into carnivore remission, I went for a routine cleaning at a dental hygienist school. An observant dentist supervising the clinic looked closely at my lower left jaw and spotted what appeared to be an apical lesion beneath an old crown on tooth&nbsp;36.

### The Jewish General Hospital: Excavating Three Hidden Cavities
Armed with this finding, I went to the dental department at the Jewish General Hospital in Montreal to have tooth 36 evaluated. The hospital clinician agreed to section and remove the old metal-fused&nbsp;crown.

When they removed the crown, they uncovered an alarming reality: **three active cavities had been rotting underneath that crown for years**, completely invisible on standard 2D dental&nbsp;x-rays!

Five dentists had examined my mouth during my three-year ordeal, and every single one of them had missed this defective crown and hidden decay. Because conventional exams only look for obvious structural failures, the bacterial toxins had continued leaking into my trigeminal nerve pathways undetected.

The dentist at the Jewish General Hospital excavated all three cavities, treated the tooth, and placed a brand-new, tightly sealed crown. Once that hidden hardware infection was permanently eliminated, the deep chronic nerve ache in my lower jaw stopped&nbsp;completely.

Combined with gentle brushing habits, my canine sensitivity settled down, leaving only mild sensitivity when I floss deeply.

## The Biophysical Takeaway: Fuel vs.&nbsp;Hardware

Looking back, my journey revealed that Burning Mouth Syndrome was not a mysterious psychosomatic curse. It was an electrochemical fire sustained by two distinct&nbsp;drivers:

1. **The Systemic Fuel (Dietary Acids & Sugars):** Plant acids directly activate acid-sensing ion channels (ASIC1 and ASIC3) and heat-pain receptors. Eliminating plant acids and sugars stopped bacterial fermentation and starved the nerve&nbsp;fire.

2. **The Dental Hardware (Infection & Tubule Exposure):** Hidden decay under tooth 36 fed bacterial toxins directly into my trigeminal nerve. Excavating the decay and replacing the crown permanently removed that physical&nbsp;spark.
