## Phase 1: The Chemical & Biological Ceasefire

### Week 1: Mapping the Wiring & Lowering the Panic
* **The Core Concept:** Pain is real, but active tissue damage is absent. Reframe the burning sensation from "my mouth is on fire or damaged" to "faulty line noise" in unmyelinated C-fibers and small sensory nerves.
* **The Reality Check:** Dropping the terror of the unknown lowers sympathetic nervous system arousal, which directly reduces pain amplification.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Set up a simple 1 to 10 daily symptom log (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
  2. Implement the "Zero-Panic Rule": when a burn spikes, verbally label it as nerve static rather than physical injury.
  3. Identify current emergency crutches (ice water, carbonation, gum, hot sauce).

### Week 2: The Secondary Checklist & Irritant Purge
* **The Core Concept:** Stop throwing gasoline on an open flame. Eliminate chemical and mechanical abrasives while auditing common medical blind spots.
* **The Reality Check:** You cannot heal an irritable mucosal barrier if daily hygiene products are stripping the surface.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Audit lab work against the core checklist: B12, ferritin, folate, zinc, fasting glucose/HbA1c, thyroid panel, and autoimmune screens.
  2. Purge oral hygiene products containing Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (SLS), whitening peroxides, essential oils, cinnamon/mint flavoring, and alcohol.
  3. Switch to a non-foaming, flavor-free or mild enzymatic toothpaste and an ultra-soft surgical toothbrush.

## Phase 2: Decoding the Rebound Trap

### Week 3: Unmasking the 72-Hour Delayed Penalty
* **The Core Concept:** The "local-minimum trap." Differentiating between what feels good for 30 seconds (acid, bubbles, spice) and what wrecks the mucosal barrier over the next 1 to 3 days.
* **The Reality Check:** Like alcohol or gaming, the brain remembers the instant relief and ignores the delayed bill. The delayed rebound fire is what drives the next craving.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Run the "3-Day Delayed Rebound Audit": track what was consumed 48 to 72 hours prior to every major pain spike.
  2. Identify specific chemical culprits: sparkling water (carbonic acid), citrus, vinegar/ferments, coffee, and energy drinks.
  3. Commit to a 7-day complete pause on high-acid drinks to test baseline mucosal tolerance.

### Week 4: The Alkaline Shield & Neutral Replacements
* **The Core Concept:** Giving the oral mucosa a chance to rebuild the salivary pellicle while satisfying the nervous system's demand for sensory input.
* **The Reality Check:** Deprivation alone fails. You must replace the destructive sensory habit with a non-damaging one.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Introduce the Sodium Bicarbonate Buffer Rinse: 1/2 teaspoon baking soda in 1 cup warm water. Swish gently for 30 seconds after eating or whenever the mouth feels acidic.
  2. Deploy non-acidic sensory substitutes: plain crushed pebble ice (cold gating via TRPM8 receptors without chemical acidity).
  3. Test neutral mucosal lubricants: plain organic coconut oil swishing or unflavored oral moisturizers.

## Phase 3: Rewiring the Cravings (SMART Recovery in Action)

### Week 5: The Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) of a Craving
* **The Core Concept:** Exposing the lopsided math. Cravings rely on the illusion that indulgence is cheap and deprivation is intolerable.
* **The Reality Check:** With BMS, the trade is catastrophic: a $2 sensory hit for a $2,000 physiological bill.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Complete a written 4-quadrant Cost-Benefit Analysis:
     * *Advantages of giving in to the trigger* (immediate distraction, taste).
     * *Disadvantages of giving in* (3 days of scalding pain, sleep disruption, frustration).
     * *Advantages of riding it out* (healing the barrier, regaining control).
     * *Disadvantages of riding it out* (15 minutes of acute restlessness).
  2. Keep a photo of this worksheet on your phone to consult during craving peaks.

### Week 6: The ABC Model & The 15-Minute Urge Surf
* **The Core Concept:** A craving is a biological wave, not an order. It crests and dissipates within 10 to 15 minutes if you don't fight it or feed it.
* **The Reality Check:** Fighting an urge drains willpower; observing it with curiosity disarms the automated dopamine loop.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Practice the ABC Tool:
     * **A (Activating Event):** 8:30 PM, mouth is burning, boredom or stress hits.
     * **B (Belief/Script):** "I need a cold beer or soda right now to stop this."
     * **C (Consequence):** Catch the script and dispute it: "I want relief, but acid will prolong this fire until Friday. I can sit with this wave."
  2. Set a literal 15-minute kitchen timer. Sit with the urge, observe the physical sensations without moving, and watch the peak pass.
  3. Increase physical friction: remove triggers from the house entirely.

## Phase 4: Self-Advocacy & Long-Term Maintenance

### Week 7: The Specialist Script & Doctor Teardown
* **The Core Concept:** Stop being dismissed as "anxious" or "stressed." Take charge of the clinical relationship with objective, structured communication.
* **The Reality Check:** General dentists and GPs often lack training in neuropathic pain. You must guide them to the right diagnostic category.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Assemble a one-page "BMS Clinical Summary": chronological onset, failed trials, secondary lab results already completed, and daily pattern.
  2. Practice the referral script to get directed to Oral Medicine or Orofacial Pain specialists: *"We have ruled out secondary causes and candidiasis; I need a formal assessment for primary peripheral neuropathy."*
  3. Learn how to constructively reject invasive dental overtreatment (unnecessary crowns, exploratory root canals).

### Week 8: The Relapse Protocol & Permanent Playbook
* **The Core Concept:** A slip is data, not a failure. Living with a sensitive nervous system requires an early-warning detection system.
* **The Reality Check:** The goal is not a life lived in fear of triggers, but a nervous system with enough margin that an accidental trigger doesn't cause a catastrophe.
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Build the "Red Flare Action Plan": exact steps to take the moment a rebound starts (baking soda rinse, crushed ice, immediate return to mild foods, zero self-blame).
  2. Pair up with an accountability partner from the cohort to maintain weekly tracking check-ins.
  3. Review the 8-week progress log: celebrate the drop in daily panic, the healing of the oral barrier, and the mastery over automatic craving loops.
