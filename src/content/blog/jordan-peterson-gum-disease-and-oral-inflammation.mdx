## What Peterson's Dentist Observed

On multiple podcasts and public interviews (including discussions with Dr. Shawn Baker and Joe Rogan), Peterson described returning to his dental hygienist and dentist after several months on strict&nbsp;carnivore:

> *"My dentist was completely stunned. For years, I had severe gum disease that was getting worse despite cleanings. When I went back, he inspected my gums and said, 'Your gum disease is completely gone. There is zero inflammation, zero bleeding, and your pocket depths have dramatically&nbsp;shrunk.'"*

For the dentist, this was an unexplainable anomaly. Dentists are trained to believe that periodontal disease is exclusively a mechanical hygiene problem: if pockets are deep and gums bleed, the patient is simply failing to floss properly or remove subgingival plaque.

Yet here was a patient who changed nothing about his mechanical brushing routine, but completely cleared his oral disease by changing the **biochemical inputs** entering his mouth.

## Why the Mouth Responds So Rapidly to Diet

To an engineer looking at biological systems, the oral cavity is a high-turnover feedback loop:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              ORAL TISSUE RESPONSE TO BIOCHEMICAL INPUTS         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  STANDARD DIET: Plant Acids (pH 3.0) + Fermentable Sugars       │
│  └── Continual acid etching + bacterial biofilm fermentation    │
│  └── Result: Chronic gingival bleeding + sensitized nerve paths │
│                                                                 │
│  ZERO ACID PROTOCOL: Ruminant Meats, Salt & Neutral Water        │
│  └── Neutral pH environment (6.8 to 7.2) + zero sugar fuel      │
│  └── Result: Rapid mucosal epithelial repair + nerve calming     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Epithelial Turnover:** Oral mucosal cells turn over every 3 to 7 days, significantly faster than skin. When you remove continuous irritants, the mucosal barrier can regenerate at an extraordinary pace.
2. **Starving Subgingival Fermentation:** Periodontal pathogens (such as *Porphyromonas gingivalis* and *Treponema denticola*) thrive in the presence of fermentable carbohydrates and acidic microenvironments. Removing refined carbs and plant sugars alters the oral microbiome within days.
3. **Elimination of Titratable Acids:** Plant-based foods, citrus, vinegars, and coffees constantly bathe the gum line in pH 2.8 to 3.5 solutions. In a compromised mouth with exposed root dentin, this constant acid exposure keeps nerve endings firing continuously.
