If dietary acids, bacterial acid fermentation, or plant anti-nutrients (like oxalates and lectins) trigger or exacerbate your Burning Mouth Syndrome (BMS), moving toward an animal-based or carnivore diet can dramatically lower direct mucosal irritation.

However, plunging immediately into strict carnivore from a standard carbohydrate diet can cause severe adaptation symptoms: electrolyte imbalances, digestive distress, and "oxalate dumping" flares. 

The most effective strategy is a structured, gradual transition across **The Proper Human Diet Spectrum**.

---

## The Proper Human Diet Spectrum Overview

Rather than viewing diet as a binary choice between "vegan" and "carnivore," view it as a continuous dial of carbohydrate and anti-nutrient elimination:

```
Low-Carb  ───►  Keto  ───►  Ketovore  ───►  Carnivore  ───►  Lion Diet
 (Stage 1)     (Stage 2)    (Stage 3)      (Stage 4)      (Stage 5)
```

By stepping down through these 5 stages over 4 to 8 weeks, you allow your gut microbiome, stomach acid production, and sensory nerve receptors to adapt smoothly while systematically testing acid removal.

---

## Stage 1: Low-Carb (Eliminate Processed Starches & Direct Soluble Acids)

**Focus:** Remove high-glycemic processed carbohydrates and aggressive dietary acids while keeping whole-food plant options.

### What to Eliminate Immediately
* All carbonated drinks, sodas, citrus fruits, vinegars, and fruit juices.
* Processed grains, breads, crackers, and refined sugar.
* High-acid sauces, hot sauces, and alcohol.

### What to Eat
* Grass-fed beef, poultry, pork, eggs, and wild-caught fish.
* Non-starchy vegetables (spinach, broccoli, zucchini, leafy greens).
* Low-sugar fruits in moderation (berries).
* Healthy fats (butter, olive oil, avocado oil).

### BMS Impact
Eliminating fast-fermentable starches starves oral anaerobic bacteria that produce lactic acid in periodontal trenches.

---

## Stage 2: Ketogenic Diet (Shift to Fat Metabolism & Zero Soluble Sugars)

**Focus:** Lower total daily net carbohydrates below 20–30 grams per day.

### What to Adjust
* Remove high-carb root vegetables (potatoes, sweet potatoes, beets).
* Remove all high-sugar fruits (bananas, apples, grapes).
* Increase fatty cuts of meat (ribeye, ground beef 80/20, lamb chops, pork belly).
* Incorporate high-fat dairy if tolerated (butter, ghee, heavy cream).

### BMS Impact
Transitioning to ketosis stabilizes blood sugar and eliminates fuel for fermentable bacterial acid production in the oral cavity.

---

## Stage 3: Ketovore (75%+ Animal Foods, Minimal Low-Oxalate Plants)

**Focus:** Transition meat into the primary centerpiece of every meal while keeping only benign, low-irritant plants for flavor or texture.

### What to Adjust
* Make meat, eggs, and animal fats 80% to 90% of your daily plate volume.
* Remove high-oxalate plants (spinach, almonds, dark chocolate, Swiss chard).
* Limit plant foods to small portions of low-toxin vegetables (squash, cucumber, peeled zucchini) or spices.

### BMS Impact
Dramatically reduces plant lectins and oxalates that irritate mucosal membranes, while giving your digestive system time to scale up bile and stomach acid production.

---

## Stage 4: Carnivore (100% Animal Kingdom Foods)

**Focus:** Complete elimination of all plant matter, seeds, grains, and vegetable oils.

### What to Eat
* Ruminant meats (beef, lamb, bison, venison).
* Non-ruminant meats (pork, poultry).
* Seafood and fish.
* Eggs and animal fats (tallow, lard, duck fat, butter).
* Salt and mineral water.

### BMS Impact
Direct contact with plant acids, seed oils, and chemical flavorings drops to absolute zero. Tubules are no longer exposed to direct dietary $H^+$ ions.

---

## Stage 5: The Lion Diet (Ruminant Meat, Salt, & Water Only)

**Focus:** The ultimate elimination protocol for extreme chemical sensitivity or stubborn BMS symptoms.

### Rules of The Lion Diet
1. **Ruminant Meat Only:** Beef, lamb, bison, goat, or elk.
2. **Pure Water:** Flat, neutral-to-alkaline tap or spring water.
3. **Pure Salt:** Non-iodized sea salt or Redmond Real Salt.

### BMS Impact
Removes histamine potential from seafood, poultry, eggs, and dairy proteins. If BMS pain drops significantly on Stage 5, you have proven a complete dietary sensitivity or chemical trigger.

---

## Crucial Tips for a Smooth BMS Transition

1. **Hydration & Electrolytes:** As insulin drops, your kidneys excrete sodium and water rapidly. Drink mineralized water and add pure sea salt to taste to avoid headaches or dry mouth flares.
2. **Digestive Support:** If fat digestion feels heavy during Stage 2 or 3, consider temporary lipase or ox bile support while your liver scales up natural bile synthesis.
3. **Log Your Pain Daily:** Record your morning, midday, and evening BMS pain score (0 to 10) as you move down each stage to identify your exact threshold of baseline relief.
