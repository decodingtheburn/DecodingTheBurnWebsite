If you're suffering from Burning Mouth Syndrome (BMS), I understand the suffering that you're going through because I had severe symptoms for three years. Along the way, there was a lot of things that I tried that didn't work, which was deeply disheartening. But through relentless persistence and some sheer luck, I stumbled across things that actually did&nbsp;work. Today, I have been 18 months pain-free (0/10 to 1/10), and I have maintained this baseline through a strict elimination protocol and dental hardware remediation (more to come later in this&nbsp;article).

*Disclaimer:* I know that what worked for me might not work for everyone. Always consult with your physician before trying anything&nbsp;new.

In this post, I want to share a bit about my journey: what worked, what didn't work, my understanding of the disease and its triggers, the root cause that I found for my own situation, and the personal literature research I conducted. Most importantly, I want to share how a targeted elimination diet can help people reduce their symptoms, and maybe for some like me, bring the burning down to a very low&nbsp;minimum.

## My Personal Mission

Connecting patients, dentists, doctors, and researchers together to find solutions that will work, and sharing information so we can make progress and discoveries into solutions for this debilitating&nbsp;condition.

## Theory on Why Low Acid and Carnivore Worked

The reason why eliminating dietary acid and fermentable carbohydrates extinguished my burning agony comes down to two direct biophysical mechanisms:

First, eliminating all plant foods removes all titratable acid from the mouth. While plant foods, fruits, vinegars, and teas continually bathe the oral cavity in acids, meat operates at a gentle, neutral pH. This allows sensitized trigeminal nerve endings and exposed cervical root surfaces to rest without constant chemical stimulation.
