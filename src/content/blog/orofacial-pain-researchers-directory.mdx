When patients suffer from intractable oral burning, temporomandibular disorders (TMD), or trigeminal neuropathies, they need more than general reassurance. They need clinician-scientists who have spent decades dissecting the neurophysiology of facial pain, conducting clinical trials, and publishing peer-reviewed research.

Because Orofacial Pain is one of dentistry's newest recognized specialties (formally recognized by the ADA in March 2020), its research community is tight-knit, highly collaborative, and spans premier academic institutions worldwide.

Below is an exhaustive master directory of the clinician-scientists who authored landmark papers, established diagnostic classifications, and lead global orofacial pain research, organized by country.

## Italy

Italian clinician-scientists have provided the most critical histological and receptor-level breakthroughs in Burning Mouth Syndrome history, moving the condition from psychosomatic speculation to physical neuropathology.

| Researcher | Primary Institution | Key Focus | Landmark Contributions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dr. Giuseppe Lauria** | Besta Neurological Institute (Milan) | Small Fiber Biopsy | Landmark *Pain* (2005) paper: performed tongue punch biopsies proving intraepithelial nerve fiber loss in BMS (conclusive small-fiber sensory neuropathy). |
| **Dr. Elisa Borsani** | University of Brescia | TRPV1 Receptors | Landmark *Histol Histopathol* (2014) paper: proved upregulation of TRPV1 (heat/capsaicin receptors) and cannabinoid receptors in tongue mucosa of BMS patients. |
| **Dr. Alessandra Majorana** | University of Brescia | Oral Mucosal Pathology | Co-investigator on mucosal innervation and epithelial receptor alterations in oral burning disorders. |
| **Dr. Antonio Scala** | University of Bologna | Classification Framework | Landmark *Crit Rev Oral Biol Med* (2003) review: established the authoritative clinical framework separating Primary BMS from Secondary BMS. |
| **Dr. Felice Femiano** | University of Naples | Therapeutic Trials | Landmark clinical trials (2002 to 2008) evaluating Alpha-Lipoic Acid (ALA) and uncovering subclinical thyroid dysfunction as a secondary burning trigger. |
| **Dr. Ambra Michelotti** | University of Naples Federico II | Muscle Physiology | Past President of the European Academy of Craniomandibular Disorders; pioneer in masticatory muscle physiology and orthodontic-TMD interactions. |
| **Dr. Daniele Manfredini** | University of Siena | Bruxism Consensus | Leading researcher in jaw biomechanics, bruxism epidemiology, and diagnostic criteria for temporomandibular joint health. |

## Landmark University Research Centers

For patients and clinicians seeking comprehensive multidisciplinary evaluations or clinical trials, these university centers represent the highest concentration of active research:

1. **Center for Oral Health Research (COHR)**
   - *Location:* University of Kentucky (Lexington, KY, USA)
   - *Key Investigators:* Dr. Jeffrey Okeson, Dr. Reny de Leeuw
   - *Core Focus:* Biomechanics, occlusal loading, and clinical management guidelines

2. **Center for Orofacial Pain**
   - *Location:* Rutgers School of Dental Medicine (Newark, NJ, USA)
   - *Key Investigators:* Dr. Rafael Benoliel, Dr. Gary Heir
   - *Core Focus:* Persistent dentoalveolar pain, trigeminal neuropathies, and nerve trauma

3. **Department of Dentistry and Oral Health**
   - *Location:* Aarhus University (Aarhus, Denmark)
   - *Key Investigators:* Dr. Peter Svensson, Dr. Lene Baad-Hansen
   - *Core Focus:* Quantitative Sensory Testing (QST), human pain models, and European pain guidelines

4. **Division of Oral Diagnostics and Rehabilitation**
   - *Location:* ACTA (Amsterdam, Netherlands)
   - *Key Investigators:* Dr. Frank Lobbezoo, Dr. Corine Visscher
   - *Core Focus:* Bruxism etiology, jaw muscle pain, and biopsychosocial screening

5. **Alan Edwards Centre for Research on Pain**
   - *Location:* McGill University (Montreal, Canada)
   - *Key Investigators:* Dr. Gilles Lavigne, Dr. Luda Diatchenko
   - *Core Focus:* Sleep architecture, genetics of pain sensitivity, and orofacial pain chronification

6. **Facial Pain Research Unit**
   - *Location:* University College London / Eastman Dental Hospital (London, UK)
   - *Key Investigators:* Prof. Joanna Zakrzewska
   - *Core Focus:* Trigeminal neuralgia, evidence-based trials, and multidisciplinary pain management

## How to Leverage This Roster

When speaking with your local dental team or seeking a specialist referral:
- **Reference the Standards:** Ask if your practitioner utilizes the **ICOP (International Classification of Orofacial Pain)** criteria to evaluate your symptoms.
- **Cite the Evidence:** Bring the landmark papers by **Lauria (2005)** and **Borsani (2014)** to demonstrate that oral burning is driven by documented peripheral nerve fibers and proton-sensitive receptors (ASIC3/TRPV1).
- **Verify Credentials:** Check the official directories at the **American Board of Orofacial Pain** (`abop.net`) and the **American Academy of Orofacial Pain** (`aaop.org`) to confirm your clinician holds dedicated, residency-backed certification.
