When patients suffer from intractable oral burning, temporomandibular disorders (TMD), or trigeminal neuropathies, they need more than general reassurance. They need clinician-scientists who have spent decades dissecting the neurophysiology of facial pain, conducting clinical trials, and publishing peer-reviewed research.

Because Orofacial Pain is one of dentistry's newest recognized specialties (formally recognized by the ADA in March 2020), its research community is tight-knit, highly collaborative, and spans premier academic institutions worldwide.

Below is an exhaustive master directory of the clinician-scientists who authored the landmark papers, established the diagnostic classifications, and lead global orofacial pain research.

## 3. Dedicated Burning Mouth Syndrome (BMS) Investigators

These researchers specifically tackled the mystery of chronic oral burning, moving the field from "psychosomatic" dismissal to proven small-fiber neurobiology:

| Researcher | Country & Institution | Breakthrough Publication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dr. Giuseppe Lauria** | Besta Neurological Institute (Italy) | *Pain (2005):* Took tongue punch biopsies proving loss of intraepithelial nerve fibers in BMS patients (conclusive small-fiber neuropathy proof). |
| **Dr. Elisa Borsani** | Univ. of Brescia (Italy) | *Histol Histopathol (2014):* Proved dramatic upregulation of TRPV1 (capsaicin/heat receptors) and cannabinoid receptors in the tongue mucosa. |
| **Dr. Päivi K. Jääskeläinen** | Univ. of Turku (Finland) | *Pain / Cephalalgia:* Brain PET and sensory mapping proving central dopaminergic hypofunction alongside peripheral nerve impairment in primary BMS. |
| **Dr. Antonio Scala** | Univ. of Bologna (Italy) | *Crit Rev Oral Biol Med (2003):* Established the formal clinical framework separating Primary BMS from Secondary BMS (local acids, hardware, systemic). |
| **Dr. Felice Femiano** | Univ. of Naples (Italy) | *Oral Surgery (2002-2008):* Landmark clinical trials on Alpha-Lipoic Acid (ALA) and uncovering subclinical hypothyroidism as an oral burning trigger. |
| **Dr. Mary Grushka** | Univ. of Toronto / Case Western (Canada/USA) | Published seminal clinical profiling from the 1980s onward on taste distortion (dysgeusia), chorda tympani disinhibition, and burning patterns. |
| **Dr. Joanna M. Zakrzewska** | Univ. College London (UK) | Cochrane reviews and clinical trial design for intractable facial pain, testing topical clonazepam, anticonvulsants, and patient coping strategies. |
| **Dr. Miriam Brennan** | Carolinas Medical Center (USA) | Past president of AAOM; leading investigator on salivary flow benchmarks, mucosal biopsies, and systemic screening protocols. |

## 4. International University Clinical Pain Centers

For patients seeking comprehensive evaluations or clinical trials, these university centers represent the highest concentration of active orofacial pain research:

1. **Center for Oral Health Research (COHR) — University of Kentucky (Lexington, KY, USA)**
   - *Leadership:* Dr. Jeffrey Okeson, Dr. Reny de Leeuw.
   - *Focus:* Biomechanics, occlusal loading, and clinical management guidelines.

2. **Center for Orofacial Pain — Rutgers School of Dental Medicine (Newark, NJ, USA)**
   - *Leadership:* Dr. Rafael Benoliel, Dr. Gary Heir.
   - *Focus:* Persistent dentoalveolar pain, trigeminal neuropathies, and nerve trauma.

3. **Department of Dentistry and Oral Health — Aarhus University (Aarhus, Denmark)**
   - *Leadership:* Dr. Peter Svensson, Dr. Lene Baad-Hansen.
   - *Focus:* Quantitative Sensory Testing (QST), human pain models, and European pain guidelines.

4. **Division of Oral Diagnostics and Rehabilitation — ACTA (Amsterdam, Netherlands)**
   - *Leadership:* Dr. Frank Lobbezoo, Dr. Corine Visscher.
   - *Focus:* Bruxism etiology, jaw muscle pain, and biopsychosocial screening.

5. **Alan Edwards Centre for Research on Pain — McGill University (Montreal, Canada)**
   - *Leadership:* Dr. Gilles Lavigne, Dr. Luda Diatchenko.
   - *Focus:* Sleep architecture, genetics of pain sensitivity, and orofacial pain chronification.

6. **Facial Pain Research Unit — University College London / Eastman Dental Hospital (UK)**
   - *Leadership:* Prof. Joanna Zakrzewska.
   - *Focus:* Trigeminal neuralgia, evidence-based trials, and multidisciplinary pain management.

## How to Leverage This Roster

When speaking with your local dental team or seeking a specialist referral:
- **Reference the Standards:** Ask if your practitioner utilizes the **ICOP (International Classification of Orofacial Pain)** criteria to evaluate your symptoms.
- **Cite the Evidence:** Bring the landmark papers by **Lauria (2005)** and **Borsani (2014)** to demonstrate that oral burning is driven by documented peripheral nerve fibers and proton-sensitive receptors (ASIC3/TRPV1).
- **Verify Credentials:** Check the official directories at the **American Board of Orofacial Pain** (`abop.net`) and the **American Academy of Orofacial Pain** (`aaop.org`) to confirm your clinician holds dedicated, residency-backed certification.
