## 🔬 Key Insights from the Data

- **Over 4 out of 5 Sufferers (81% Combined):** When combining the core **75% Yes** response with the **6% custom write-in "agree with this"**, an overwhelming **81% majority** of BMS sufferers confirm that dietary acidity directly triggers or heightens their oral burning symptoms.
- **Biochemical Mechanism:** Acidic beverages (pH 2.5 - 4.5) dissolve the outer smear layer and penetrate micro-fissures in enamel/dentine or sensitized mucosal receptors (TRPV1 / ASIC channels), firing continuous nociceptive pain signals to the brain.
