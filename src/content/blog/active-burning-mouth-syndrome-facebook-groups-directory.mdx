When dealing with Burning Mouth Syndrome (BMS), finding an active patient community is often the first step out of isolation. 

Because general dentists and GPs frequently lack specialized training in neuropathic oral pain, peer-led Facebook groups have become major hubs where thousands of patients share daily symptoms, compare lab tests, discuss elimination diets, and recommend open-minded clinicians.

Whether you are looking for English-speaking cohorts or French-speaking (*glossodynie* / *stomatodynie*) support networks, here is a curated directory of the most active communities.

---

## Why Joining a BMS Facebook Group Helps

1. **Crowdsourced Symptom Pattern Matching:** Discovering that others share your exact 72-hour delayed burning spikes or sensitivity to dietary acids validates your experience.
2. **Clinician Recommendations:** Group members regularly share names of Oral Medicine specialists, neuro-dentists, and independent radiologists in their geographic area.
3. **Real-Time Trial Feedback:** Read first-hand experiences on elimination protocols (such as low-acid or [carnivore elimination baselines](/blog/1-my-bms-18-months-carnivore-experiment/)), non-SLS toothpastes, and topical buffering rinses.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of BMS Communities

* **Search Before Posting:** Use the group's search bar to look up specific keywords (e.g., *"B12"*, *"capsaicin"*, *"SLS-free"*, *"carnivore"*, or *"oral medicine"*) before starting a new thread.
* **Keep a Neutral Mindset:** Patient communities contain a wide spectrum of experiences. Focus on empirical data, structured elimination tests, and verifiable diagnostic steps rather than panic-inducing posts.
* **Share What Works:** If a specific low-acid trial or dental adjustment lowers your pain score, post your protocol to help fellow members navigate their own recovery.
