When searching for the source of oral acidity, most people look at what enters through their lips: coffee, citrus fruits, vinegar dressings, and soft&nbsp;drinks.

Yet for a significant group of BMS sufferers, the primary acid source travels in the opposite direction: rising upward from the stomach through **gastroesophageal reflux (GERD)** or **laryngopharyngeal reflux (LPR)**.

Often called "silent reflux," this subterranean acid flow can deposit corrosive gastric juices and digestive enzymes right onto delicate gums, exposed tooth roots, and the tongue, keeping pain receptors perpetually&nbsp;ignited.

## The Mechanics of Silent Reflux (LPR) in the Mouth

Unlike classic heartburn, which causes severe chest burning, silent reflux frequently produces zero indigestion or stomach pain. 

Instead, microscopic droplets of hydrochloric acid and stomach enzymes (specifically **pepsin**) vaporize or travel upward while you are upright or asleep, settling directly into the throat and oral&nbsp;cavity:

## Clues That Reflux May Be Feeding Your Burning

Because silent reflux rarely causes typical heartburn, looking for subtle head, neck, and dental signs can help connect the dots:

* **Morning Symptoms:** Waking up with a sour or metallic taste, a dry coated tongue, or a hoarse voice that clears after an hour.
* **Frequent Throat Clearing:** Feeling a persistent "lump" in your throat (globus sensation) or chronic post-nasal drip without allergies.
* **Palatal Enamel Erosion:** Dentists noticing thinning enamel on the inside surfaces of your upper front teeth (the classic erosion pattern of gastric acid).
* **Lingual Papillae Redness:** Irritated, swollen red taste buds at the back of the tongue where rising acid makes first contact with the oral&nbsp;cavity.

## How Neutralizing Reflux Fits the Zero-Acid Protocol

In my own experience, keeping the oral cavity at a neutral pH was essential to stopping my symptoms. 

If gastric acid is continually rising into your mouth, even a strict zero-acid diet can struggle to bring complete relief because the acid is being generated internally. Addressing reflux through meal timing (not eating within 3 to 4 hours of sleeping), elevating the head of the bed, and eliminating gut-irritating fermentable carbohydrates helps seal off the bottom-up acid leak.
