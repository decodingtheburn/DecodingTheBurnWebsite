## 🔬 The Biology: Why Class V Restorations Fall Off

In healthy young dentin, the tissue is permeable and packed with open, fluid-filled tubules. When a dentist etches normal dentin, the acid opens these tubule apertures, allowing liquid bonding resin to flow inside and create deep **resin tags** and a strong **hybrid layer**.

However, when tooth roots are exposed to chronic friction, acid erosion, or gum recession over decades, the tooth defends itself through a process called **sclerosis**.

### 1. Sclerotic Dentin Acts Like a Sheet of Glass
The pulp lays down mineral crystals (hypermineralization) directly inside the tubule openings, sealing them shut. Instead of a porous, sponge-like surface that easily absorbs adhesive, **sclerotic dentin turns into a shiny, dark, acid-resistant sheet of glass**.

### 2. Mild "Self-Etch" Adhesives Cannot Penetrate It
Many modern dental practices rely on one-bottle "universal" or "self-etch" adhesives to minimize post-operative sensitivity. However, mild self-etch primers have a weak pH that **cannot etch or break through the hypermineralized sclerotic surface**. The adhesive sits loosely on top like tape stuck to a dusty window, leading to inevitable bond failure within weeks or months.

### 3. Dentists Mistakenly Fear Etching Deep Dentin
Many dentists refrain from aggressively etching cervical lesions out of fear of causing nerve sensitivity. But biologically, **sclerotic dentin tubules are already mineralized and plugged shut**. Hydrodynamic fluid movement is naturally blocked, meaning sclerotic dentin is far less sensitive to phosphoric acid etching than normal healthy dentin.

## 🛠️ The Bulletproof Clinical Protocol: What Your Dentist MUST Do

If you need a Class V restoration at the gum line, you must choose a dentist who understands adhesive biomaterials and does not rely on quick "one-bottle" shortcuts. The literature requires a strict 5-step protocol:

1. **Strict Moisture Isolation:** Using a rubber dam or retraction cord to eliminate gingival fluid and blood contamination.
2. **Mechanical Roughening (Disrupting the Glass):** Lightly dusting the shiny sclerotic surface with a fine diamond burr or 50-micron aluminum oxide airbrasion for 5 seconds. Research shows this step alone **increases bond strength by over 200%**.
3. **Phosphoric Acid Etching (37%):** Etching the enamel margins and the sclerotic dentin for 15 seconds to break through the hypermineralized barrier.
4. **Scrubbing Universal Adhesive (Etch-and-Rinse Mode):** Vigorous 20-second active scrubbing of the adhesive into the micro-porosities, followed by thorough air-thinning to evaporate solvent.
5. **Flexible or Heated Composite Placement:** Using highly filled flowable or warm composite to adapt seamlessly to the high-stress cervical margin.

## 🥩 The Carnivore Connection: Why a Zero-Acid Diet Changes Everything

What happens if a cervical filling pops off, or if you have exposed dentinal tubules that have not yet been restored?

In a standard diet filled with coffee, fruit juices, sodas, tomatoes, and fermentable carbohydrates, **dietary acids constantly bathe the exposed gum-line tubules**. These acids dissolve protective mucin/smear plugs and pull on intradental nerve fibers, triggering relentless Trigeminal firing and escalating Burning Mouth Syndrome symptoms.

### How a Zero-Acid Carnivore Diet Protects Exposed Tubules:
- **Zero Acid Contact:** Eliminating plant acids (citric, malic, oxalic) and acidic beverages removes the chemical irritant that destabilizes tubule fluid.
- **Zero Bacterial Lactic Acid:** Removing sugars and starches starves oral bacteria like *Streptococcus mutans*, halting localized acid production at the gum line.
- **Natural Remineralization:** Without constant acid demineralization, natural salivary calcium and phosphate continuously mineralize and plug exposed tubule apertures.

When dietary acidity is reduced to zero, exposed root dentin and compromised Class V margins no longer trigger the Acid-Sensing Ion Channels (ASICs) in pulpal and lingual nerves—allowing oral pain to calm down even while you search for an evidence-based dentist.
