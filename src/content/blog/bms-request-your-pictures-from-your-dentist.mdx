At the absolute height of my Burning Mouth Syndrome, I was living in so much continuous, agonizing pain that parts of those months are almost a blur. 

During one of my urgent dental visits, the clinical team took detailed high-resolution intraoral photos. I was in so much distress at the time that I didn't even remember they took them until later when I requested my complete dental records.

When I received my file and opened the images, there it was: clear evidence of **gingival inflammation, marginal redness, and tissue irritation** right around the gumline and cervical borders.

## 🔍 Why You Must Request Your Dental Intraoral Photos

When you are suffering from severe oral burning, doctors and dentists frequently look at your mouth for 15 seconds and say: *"Everything looks completely normal."*

However, high-resolution intraoral camera photography captures subtle details that the naked eye under quick examination often misses:
- **Marginal Redness & Erythema:** Micro-inflammation along the free gingival margin.
- **Interdental Papillae Swelling:** Vascular congestion and tissue engorgement between teeth.
- **Baseline Comparative Record:** A visual benchmark to compare against once you initiate targeted anti-inflammatory protocols.

### 📌 Key Takeaway for BMS Patients
Do not rely on memory alone. Request your full chart, radiograph records, and **all intraoral photographs** taken by your dentist. Having high-resolution visual evidence is essential for tracking your progress from peak pain down to complete remission.
