If you search the medical literature or browse online health forums, you will find hundreds of articles written *about* chronic oral burning and autoimmune mucosal destruction by dentists, oral medicine specialists, and rheumatologists. They list textbook diagnostic criteria, order standard antibody panels, and prescribe off-label nerve blockers or artificial saliva&nbsp;substitutes.

Almost none of those clinicians have ever felt that unremitting physical fire in their own&nbsp;tissues.

There are two extraordinary exceptions: **Dr. Neli Nikolova**, an MD who developed Burning Mouth Syndrome herself and lectures fellow doctors on the condition, and **Dr. Kara Wada**, an immunologist living with Sjögren's disease who actively breaks down emerging clinical research and pipeline&nbsp;medications.

When medical doctors cross the boundary from provider to patient, the clinical conversation changes completely. They know firsthand that symptoms are not "in your head," and they use their clinical authority to challenge medical&nbsp;dogma.

## 1. Dr. Neli Nikolova, MD: The Physician Who Got&nbsp;BMS

* **Medical Specialty**: General Medicine, Midlife Health &amp; Menopause Specialist
* **Public Profile &amp; Facebook**: [facebook.com/neli.nikolova.169](https://www.facebook.com/neli.nikolova.169)
* **Key Contribution**: Landmark lecture on *"Burning Mouth Syndrome in Postmenopausal Women"* at the Emirates Medical Association (EMA) OB/GYN Congress

Dr. Neli Nikolova (also referenced internationally as Dr. Neli Neikova) is a practicing physician specializing in midlife women's health who chose to speak openly about navigating Burning Mouth Syndrome as a patient&nbsp;herself.

### The Physician-as-Patient Reality Check
When everyday patients consult a doctor with agonizing oral burning, they are routinely dismissed because the oral mucosa looks pink, moist, and free of ulcers. When Dr. Nikolova developed BMS, that same dismissive curtain dropped in front of&nbsp;her.

She understood human physiology thoroughly, yet when burning mouth struck, visual examinations showed normal-looking tissue. Experiencing severe, 24/7 oral burning in a visually "normal" mouth proved beyond doubt that BMS is an objective neuro-endocrine breakdown, not a psychological&nbsp;flaw.

### Educating Clinicians from the Inside
Rather than suffering silently, Dr. Nikolova took her experience directly to medical congresses. At the Emirates Medical Association (EMA) OB/GYN Congress, she presented a dedicated lecture on postmenopausal BMS, educating physicians&nbsp;on:
* **The Estrogen-Nerve Link**: How declining hormone levels rob oral sensory nerves of neuro-protective signaling, lowering sensory firing thresholds.
* **Autonomic &amp; Metabolic Control**: Why blood sugar dips, prolonged fasting intervals, and nervous system dysregulation amplify mucosal hyperalgesia.
* **Ending Medical Gaslighting**: Urging fellow clinicians to stop writing off oral burning as "anxiety," validating that oral nerve pain is an early warning sign of systemic&nbsp;dysfunction.

## 2. Dr. Kara Wada, MD: The Immunologist Tackling Sjögren's &amp; Pipeline&nbsp;Meds

* **Medical Specialty**: Quadruple Board-Certified Pediatric &amp; Adult Allergy/Immunology and Lifestyle Medicine Physician, Associate Professor at The Ohio State University
* **Website &amp; Educational Platform**: [drkarawada.com](https://drkarawada.com/)
* **Podcast**: *Becoming Immune Confident*
* **Lived Experience**: Diagnosed with Sjögren's Disease, Mast Cell Activation Syndrome (MCAS), and Small Fiber Neuropathy

While Dr. Nikolova addresses primary neuropathic BMS, **Dr. Kara Wada** represents the front line of secondary mucosal and autoimmune oral destruction. Dr. Wada is a practicing academic immunologist who lives with **Sjögren's disease**, an autoimmune condition that attacks the body's moisture-producing salivary and lacrimal&nbsp;glands.

### The Direct Bridge to Burning Mouth Symptoms
Sjögren's disease is one of the primary medical conditions clinicians must rule out when evaluating burning mouth complaints. When autoimmune inflammation impairs the salivary glands:
1. **Loss of the Salivary Pellicle**: Saliva is not just water; it is a complex protective shield of mucins, histatins, and antimicrobial proteins. Without adequate salivary flow, oral tissues lose their lubricating barrier, leaving raw mucosal nerve endings exposed to microscopic friction and acidic&nbsp;erosion.
2. **Small Fiber Neuropathy Overlap**: Sjögren's does not stop at dryness. It frequently causes systemic small fiber neuropathy, the exact same peripheral nerve dysfunction that characterizes primary Burning Mouth&nbsp;Syndrome.

### Breaking Down Research &amp; Pipeline Medications
Historically, patients with Sjögren's and severe xerostomia were offered little more than symptom palliatives: lozenges, artificial saliva sprays, or pilocarpine. Dr. Wada has utilized her dual perspective as an immunologist and patient to educate the chronic illness community about **the next generation of pipeline therapies entering clinical&nbsp;trials**:
* **Targeted B-Cell Biologics**: Following trial data on novel disease-modifying agents (such as ianalumab and telitacicept) that target B-cell activating factors to halt autoimmune destruction of glandular tissue before irreversible fibrosis&nbsp;occurs.
* **CD40-Ligand Antagonists**: Breaking down clinical developments surrounding T-cell/B-cell co-stimulation inhibitors (like dazodalibep) designed to calm systemic immune hyperactivity.
* **Empowered Self-Advocacy**: Through her *Becoming Immune Confident* platform, Dr. Wada teaches patients how to track objective biomarkers, prepare for specialist consultations, and advocate for themselves when symptoms are dismissed as "just dry&nbsp;mouth."

## Why Physician-Patients Matter for the BMS&nbsp;Community

Physicians are trained in a medical culture that discourages public vulnerability. Admitting to an invisible illness that textbooks still classify as "incurable" or "idiopathic" carries significant professional&nbsp;stigma.

When physicians like **Dr. Neli Nikolova** and **Dr. Kara Wada** step forward, they shatter that culture of silence:
1. **They Validate the Biology**: When an immunologist or medical doctor confirms that their own nerves and tissues are burning, it exposes the absurdity of calling these conditions "imaginary."
2. **They Follow the Science, Not the Dogma**: Because they read the research through the lens of lived suffering, they are the first to look beyond standard symptom-masking drugs and evaluate emerging pipeline&nbsp;mechanisms.
3. **They Transform Patient Agency**: They provide patients with the clinical vocabulary, diagnostic tests, and scientific frameworks needed to walk into a doctor's office as informed partners rather than dismissed&nbsp;sufferers.

Their leadership proves that whether the fire stems from neuro-endocrine dysregulation, exposed dentinal tubules, or autoimmune glandular breakdown, the solution begins with taking the patient's pain seriously.
