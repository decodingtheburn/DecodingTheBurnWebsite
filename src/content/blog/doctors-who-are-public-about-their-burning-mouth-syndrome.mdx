When everyday patients describe an agonizing, round-the-clock fire in their tongue, gums, or palate, they are routinely dismissed. Because the oral mucosa appears visually pink, moist, and free of visible ulcerations, conventional clinicians often shrug, order basic blood panels, and suggest that the burning is "just stress or&nbsp;anxiety."

That dismissive illusion shatters when healthcare professionals develop Burning Mouth Syndrome&nbsp;themselves.

When a physician, dentist, or specialist experiences the excruciating reality of neuropathic oral burning firsthand, they realize the enormous gap between standard clinical textbooks and real-world physiology. They know their symptoms are not psychosomatic. They know what it feels like to sit across from colleagues who have no answers, and they have the clinical vocabulary to speak out and challenge medical&nbsp;dogma.

Below is an open directory of doctors, dentists, and clinicians who have publicly shared their personal journey or dedicated clinical focus with Burning Mouth&nbsp;Syndrome.

## 1. Dr. Neli Nikolova, MD (Physician &amp; Menopause Specialist)

* **Specialty**: General Medicine, Midlife Health &amp; Menopause Specialist
* **Public Profile &amp; Facebook**: [facebook.com/neli.nikolova.169](https://www.facebook.com/neli.nikolova.169)
* **Clinical Focus**: Neuro-endocrine factors, postmenopausal BMS, autonomic nervous system stability

Dr. Neli Nikolova (also referenced in international medical forums as Dr. Neli Neikova) is a medical doctor specializing in midlife women's health who has spoken openly about navigating Burning Mouth Syndrome as a patient&nbsp;herself.

Experiencing BMS firsthand gave Dr. Nikolova direct insight into the isolating diagnostic maze that patients face every day. Despite severe oral burning and scalding sensations, routine physical visual exams of the oral cavity revealed completely normal-looking mucosa, highlighting why conventional medicine so often fails to recognize neuropathic&nbsp;disorders.

Rather than accepting the condition as an untreatable mystery, Dr. Nikolova has taken her personal and clinical insights directly to medical congresses. She delivered a featured lecture titled *"Burning Mouth Syndrome in Postmenopausal Women"* at the Emirates Medical Association (EMA) OB/GYN Congress, teaching fellow physicians about the complex interplay between estrogen depletion, nervous system hypersensitivity, blood sugar fluctuations, and mucosal nerve&nbsp;regulation.

By speaking out publicly on her Facebook platform and at international medical conferences, Dr. Nikolova has become a leading physician advocate bridging the divide between clinical science and lived patient&nbsp;misery.

## 2. Dr. Miriam Grushka, DDS, PhD (Oral Medicine Specialist &amp; Pioneer Researcher)

* **Specialty**: Certified Specialist in Oral Medicine (Toronto, Canada), MSc, PhD
* **Website &amp; Clinic**: [miriamgrushka.ca](https://www.miriamgrushka.ca/)
* **Clinical Focus**: Orofacial pain, trigeminal neuropathic dysesthesia, chorda tympani taste alterations

Dr. Miriam Grushka is widely regarded as one of the world's most dedicated clinical authorities on Burning Mouth Syndrome. For more than three decades, Dr. Grushka has stood on the front lines of patient advocacy, publishing groundbreaking peer-reviewed research proving that BMS is a genuine small-fiber neuropathy rather than a psychological&nbsp;complaint.

Throughout her career, Dr. Grushka has treated thousands of BMS sufferers from across the globe. Her pioneering clinical papers established the foundational link between taste nerve damage (chorda tympani hypofunction) and the release of trigeminal sensory inhibition that triggers chronic burning sensations. She also popularized the clinical use of topical dissolvable clonazepam for oral neuropathic&nbsp;hyperalgesia.

Dr. Grushka has consistently spoken out at medical and dental conferences about the failure of standard dental training to teach orofacial neuropathy, encouraging clinicians to listen to their patients rather than dismissing&nbsp;them.

## 3. Dr. Louise Newson, MB ChB, FRCGP (General Practitioner &amp; Menopause Specialist)

* **Specialty**: General Practitioner, Founder of Newson Health &amp; the Balance Platform (UK)
* **Website**: [newsonhealth.co.uk](https://www.newsonhealth.co.uk/)
* **Clinical Focus**: Hormonal deficiency, oral mucosal estrogen receptors, neuro-inflammation

Dr. Louise Newson is one of the United Kingdom's most prominent physicians advocating for women's midlife health. Through her clinic, podcasts, and public educational articles, Dr. Newson has frequently highlighted Burning Mouth Syndrome as one of the most agonizing, yet under-recognized, manifestations of hormone&nbsp;deficiency.

Dr. Newson regularly shares clinical case stories of women who spent years seeing dentists, periodontists, and neurologists without relief. She educates healthcare providers on the fact that oral tissues are densely packed with estrogen and progesterone receptors. When hormone levels decline sharply during perimenopause and menopause, mucosal cellular repair diminishes and sensory nerve thresholds drop, leaving oral tissues vulnerable to chronic burning&nbsp;dysesthesia.

Her public work has helped thousands of women connect their oral pain to systemic endocrine shifts and seek comprehensive medical&nbsp;support.

## 4. Dr. Nathalie Rei, DMD, MSc (Stomatologist &amp; Oral Medicine Specialist, CHUM)

* **Specialty**: Oral Medicine &amp; Stomatology, Associate Professor at Université de Montréal
* **Clinical Center**: [Centre Hospitalier de l'Université de Montréal (CHUM)](https://stomatologiemontreal.com/)
* **Clinical Focus**: Oral mucosal diseases, mechanical allodynia, trigeminal neuropathic pain

Dr. Nathalie Rei is a clinical stomatologist at the CHUM hospital in Montreal and an associate professor training the next generation of dental specialists. She is one of the rare oral medicine clinicians who actively tests for and documents mechanical allodynia in patients suffering from burning&nbsp;mouth.

During my own forensic examination, Dr. Rei methodically tested my oral mucosa and canine teeth, documenting severe 10/10 flossing allodynia on tooth #23 and generalized quadrant hyperalgesia across trigeminal branches V2 and V3. While many dental practitioners dismiss flossing sensitivity as minor gingival irritation, Dr. Rei explicitly identified that my sensory nerves were firing in an up-regulated neuropathic&nbsp;state.

In her academic lectures to maxillofacial residents, Dr. Rei highlights the critical distinction between visible mucosal pathology and invisible neuropathic sensitization, teaching clinicians to validate the physical reality of oral nerve&nbsp;pain.

## 5. Dr. Gary Klasser, DDS (Professor of Oral Medicine, LSU School of Dentistry)

* **Specialty**: Orofacial Pain and Oral Medicine Specialist, Louisiana State University
* **Profile &amp; Academic Work**: [LSU Health Sciences Center](https://www.lsuhsc.edu/)
* **Clinical Focus**: Orofacial pain classification, trigeminal neuropathies, diagnostic standards for BMS

Dr. Gary Klasser is a leading international researcher and co-author of international diagnostic criteria for Burning Mouth Syndrome. Through numerous published articles and continuing medical education lectures, Dr. Klasser has fought against the historical stigma that labeled BMS patients as "neurotic" or&nbsp;"anxious."

Dr. Klasser regularly educates practicing dentists on the biological mechanisms of BMS, illustrating how peripheral nerve fiber density changes in the tongue and how central pain modulation pathways become impaired. His lectures provide clinicians with clear diagnostic algorithms to systematically rule out secondary triggers (nutritional deficiencies, contact allergies, fungal infections, and mechanical friction) before classifying pain as primary&nbsp;neuropathy.

## 6. Peggy Sharr, LCSW (Clinician &amp; Author of *The BMS Survival Guide*)

* **Specialty**: Licensed Clinical Social Worker &amp; Psychotherapist
* **Website**: [peggysharr.com](https://peggysharr.com/)
* **Book**: *The Burning Mouth Syndrome Survival Guide*

While not a medical doctor, Peggy Sharr is a licensed mental health clinician who developed Burning Mouth Syndrome herself and became one of the first healthcare professionals to write a book documenting her personal battle and recovery&nbsp;framework.

Her published guide bridges the gap between clinical psychotherapy and daily patient survival. She details the trauma of medical gaslighting, how to methodically track food acidity and dental triggers, and how to calm the central nervous system during severe flare-ups. Her work remains an essential resource for patients looking for practical, experience-backed survival&nbsp;strategies.
