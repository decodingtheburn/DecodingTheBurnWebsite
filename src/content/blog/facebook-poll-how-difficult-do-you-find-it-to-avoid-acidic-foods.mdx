This is a temporary draft post for an upcoming community Facebook poll investigating the dietary and behavioral challenges of avoiding acidic foods when managing BMS.

## Poll Question
*"For those sensitive to acid, how difficult do you find it to avoid those foods that you know of?"*

### Poll Options:
* [ ] **Extremely Difficult** (I struggle to cut out coffee, fruit, or favorite foods completely)
* [ ] **Moderately Difficult** (I manage most of the time, but social eating or cravings cause slip-ups)
* [ ] **Easy / Strict Protocol** (Once I realized acid was the trigger, eliminating it was straightforward)

---

## Why We Are Asking
Eliminating soluble dietary acids (coffee, citrus, tomatoes, wine) and fermentable carbohydrates requires a major lifestyle and psychological shift. Understanding how difficult patients find this elimination protocol helps us design better ramp-down strategies and practical food substitution guides.
